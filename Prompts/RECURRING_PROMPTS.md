# RECURRING PROMPTS

## 1. Positioning & Messaging

### 1.1 — Positioning Pressure Test
```
I need to pressure-test a positioning direction for Startbutton.

Current framing: [paste or describe the positioning]
Target audience: [founders / COOs / heads of expansion / other]
Context: [where this will appear — sales deck / website / sales conversation / etc.]

Run it through these questions:
- Does this reflect the buyer's reality more clearly than they currently articulate it?
- What's the hidden assumption in this framing?
- What does a skeptical, well-informed prospect say back?
- What's the educational gap this leaves unaddressed?

End with a sharper version and flag what changed.
```

---

### 1.2 — Messaging From Scratch
```
Help me develop messaging for [product / feature / campaign / audience segment].

Context:
- Audience: [who specifically]
- Their constraint or trigger: [what's happening in their world that makes this relevant]
- What we want them to understand or do: [outcome]
- Tone: [calm and precise / direct / narrative-led]

Lead with the core message, then give me 3 variations — one more conceptual, one more operational, one more specific to a named constraint.
```

---

### 1.3 — Category Framing
```
I'm trying to sharpen how we define and explain [the MoR category / a specific product / a market dynamic].

Current explanation: [paste what we're using now]
Audience sophistication: [high / low / mixed]
Main confusion or objection we keep encountering: [describe it]

Help me reframe this so that:
- The problem is visible before the solution
- The buyer feels recognized, not educated at
- The category feels inevitable, not invented

Give me a short version (2–3 sentences) and a medium version (short paragraph).
```

## 2. LinkedIn & Thought Leadership

### 2.1 — LinkedIn Post Draft
```
Write a LinkedIn post on the following topic:

Topic / angle: [what you want to say]
What triggered this: [observation, conversation, data point, frustration]
Core insight or tension: [the non-obvious thing]
Target reader: [founders expanding into Africa / operators / fintech builders / etc.]

Voice requirements:
- Grounded, clear, slightly analytical — not inspirational or performative
- Lead with the tension or observation, not a hook question
- No bullet-point listicles unless the format genuinely serves the idea
- No buzzwords, no "excited to share," no forced profundity
- End with a clear point of view, not a question to drive engagement

Aim for 150–250 words. Give me one version, then flag what you'd tighten.
```

---

### 2.2 — Thought Leadership Angle Development
```
I want to develop a thought leadership angle around [topic / observation / market dynamic].

What I know or believe: [rough thinking]
What most people in this space get wrong or miss: [the gap]
Why this matters now: [timing or trigger]

Help me:
1. Sharpen the central claim into one sentence
2. Identify the strongest supporting argument
3. Name the counterargument worth acknowledging
4. Suggest 2–3 formats this could take (LinkedIn post / newsletter / long essay / talk)
```

## 3. Strategy & Problem Diagnosis

### 3.1 — Problem Diagnosis
```
I'm trying to diagnose what's actually wrong with [situation / campaign / GTM motion / team dynamic].

What's visible: [the symptom or result]
What's been tried: [any solutions already attempted]
What changed recently (if anything): [context shift]

Work through this in layers:
1. Is this a strategy / framing problem?
2. Is this a distribution / reach problem?
3. Is this an execution problem?
4. Is this an alignment problem?
5. Is this a timing / market readiness problem?

Surface the most likely root cause and the assumption most worth questioning. Then give me one leverage point.
```

---

### 3.2 — Strategic Options Analysis
```
I'm weighing a decision on [describe the choice].

Context:
- What we're trying to achieve: [outcome]
- Current constraints: [team, budget, time, market]
- Options I'm considering: [list them or ask me to generate them]

For each option, give me:
- The core logic (why it could work)
- The key assumption it depends on
- The most likely failure mode
- Second-order effects

End with a clear recommendation and what would change it.
```

---

### 3.3 — GTM Motion Review
```
Review our current GTM motion for Startbutton.

What I'll share: [paste current motion description or campaign plan]

Evaluate it against:
- Is the ICP precise enough?
- Is the message reflecting a real buyer constraint or an internal narrative?
- Does the channel match buyer behavior at the moment of need?
- Where is the trust gap — and how does the motion address it?
- Does this compound or is it a one-off?

Flag the weakest point first, then give me the highest-leverage fix.
```

## 4. Writing & Editorial

### 4.1 — Long-Form Draft (Newsletter / Essay)
```
Help me write a piece for [Questing newsletter / external publication / internal doc].

Topic: [what it's about]
Core argument or insight: [the thing I want readers to walk away understanding]
Audience: [operators building in Africa / fintech founders / etc.]
Tone: [analytical and narrative / more conversational / more reported]
Length: [~600 words / ~1000 words / longer]

Structure it so that:
- The opening creates curiosity without being a teaser
- The underlying dynamic becomes visible mid-piece
- It ends with a clear implication, not a vague "food for thought"

Draft in full, then flag the weakest section.
```

---

### 4.2 — Interview Prep (Book / Podcast / Profile)
```
Help me prepare for an interview with [name / role / context].

About them: [who they are, what they've built or know]
What I'm trying to get from this conversation: [specific knowledge, narrative confirmation, a source quote, a perspective]
What I already know or have documented: [context]
For the MTN Nigeria book specifically: [flag chapter relevance if applicable]

Give me:
1. 5–7 core questions in priority order
2. 2–3 follow-up probes for the most important question
3. One tension worth surfacing that they may not volunteer
```

---

### 4.3 — Internal Brief or Strategy Doc
```
Help me write a [brief / strategy doc / team memo] on [topic].

Audience: [leadership / marketing team / cross-functional stakeholders]
What they need to understand or decide after reading it: [outcome]
Key context they have vs don't have: [calibration]
Length: [short memo / medium brief / full strategy doc]

Structure it as:
1. Core situation or problem
2. Key analysis or options
3. Recommendation with reasoning
4. What I need from them (if anything)

Be direct. No filler transitions. Flag where you've made assumptions.
```

## 5. Team & Execution

### 5.1 — Delegation Brief
```
Help me write a delegation brief for [team member / role] on [task or initiative].

What they need to execute: [describe the work]
Why it matters (the intent, not just the task): [context]
What's fixed vs flexible: [constraints and degrees of freedom]
What good looks like: [success criteria]
What I'm not delegating (where I stay involved): [boundaries]

Keep it tight. They should be able to read this once and execute without needing to come back for clarification on intent.
```

---

### 5.2 — Feedback Structuring
```
Help me structure feedback on [piece of work / output / behavior].

Situation: [what was produced or what happened]
What's working: [what should be preserved]
What's not working: [the specific issue]
Why it matters: [connect to the outcome, not just the preference]

Format it so it's:
- Specific (not vague)
- Connected to the goal (not just my preference)
- Actionable (clear what to do differently)
- Not more than 3 main points
```

## 6. Research & Analysis

### 6.1 — Market or Competitive Analysis
```
Analyze [market dynamic / competitor / trend] through the lens of African fintech infrastructure.

What I already know or believe: [context]
What I'm trying to understand: [specific question]
What I'll do with this: [decision it informs]

Key filters to apply:
- Does this account for African market structure (fragmentation, trust dynamics, distribution reality)?
- Is this actually differentiated or just "sounds smart"?
- What's the second-order effect?

Give me signal, not just summary. Tell me what's actually important and what the conventional view is missing.
```

---

### 6.2 — Audience or ICP Analysis
```
Help me sharpen the ICP for [Startbutton / a specific product / a campaign].

Current assumption about who we're targeting: [describe]
What we know about their constraints and triggers: [context]
Where the current definition feels too broad or imprecise: [the gap]

Give me:
1. A tighter ICP definition (role, company stage, expansion trigger)
2. The specific moment of constraint when they'd engage
3. What they're likely already trying (or tolerating) before finding us
4. The message that would feel like it was written for them
```
