# Meeting Follow-up (Chief of Staff)

*Master copy: github.com/Danieltadeyemi/daniel-os, `.claude/skills/meeting-followup/SKILL.md`. Keep both in step when this changes.*

Runs weekdays ~6pm (Africa/Lagos).

## Find
Google Drive (Startbutton account) docs titled `… - Notes by Gemini` created today. Each has Summary, Decisions, Next steps (`[Name] Task: …`), Details with timestamps, Transcript.

## Sort into four buckets
1. **Daniel's tasks** — next steps naming Daniel, or clearly assigned to marketing's lead. Propose adding to his standup section with a due date. **Daniel approves before anything is added.**
2. **Marketing team tasks** — items for Dami or the content marketer. Check they appear in their standup section; flag if missing (feeds Meeting Prep nudges).
3. **Waiting on others** — items other teams owe marketing (e.g. Ayomide updating the onboarding form). Track; flag when 5+ working days old.
4. **Learnings, decisions, bigger issues** — log to `Startbutton/issues-and-decisions.md`.
   - *Decision:* what was agreed, by whom, date, link to the notes doc.
   - *Learning:* one-line insight; if it's post-worthy, also suggest it for the Brand Editor Vault.
   - *Issue:* risk, escalation, cross-team friction, CEO ask, or anything that needs Daniel's call. Stays **Open** until Daniel marks it resolved. Open issues appear in the Morning Brief and Friday close-out.

## Report to Daniel (one message)
- Meetings he missed: 2-line catch-up each.
- Proposed tasks to add (approve / edit / skip).
- New issues needing his call.
Nothing else if the day was quiet.

## Confidentiality
The log lives in Daniel's personal repo. Write **summaries in his own working shorthand only**: no transcript excerpts, no customer names or personal data, no financials beyond what's needed to act. Link to the Drive doc for detail.

## Never
Add tasks to anyone's standup without approval · message other teams · copy transcripts into the repo.
