# Thought Leadership (Brand Editor)

Cadence: one post every two weeks (alternates with Campaign of the Week).
Lighter version of `ContentMachine/CLAUDE.md`: keep the Vault, interview and learning loop; drop the 9/10 council loop.

1. **Oracle (weekly, automatic):** mine Slack, Notion, Drive and the Startbutton inbox for specific moments worth writing about (the filling-station post is the model). Score and add to `ContentMachine/vault/ideas.md` with source.
2. **Interview (Daniel, ~10 min):** pick one Vault idea; ask 3–4 questions; Daniel types or dictates. Save the raw answers to `ContentMachine/vault/posts/<slug>/raw.md`. **His words are sacred: never paraphrase the raw file.**
3. **Draft + one review pass:** draft from the raw file, style guide and lessons; one critique-and-fix round; present 2 directions when the angle is open.
4. **Learning loop:** after Daniel posts, compare draft with final, add a rule to `content-lessons.md`.

Goal of natural writing: lead with a specific moment, one idea per post, earn the insight at the end, write like texting a smart friend.

## Voice (read before drafting)
- `ContentMachine/context/style-guide.md` then `ContentMachine/context/content-lessons.md` (lessons override the guide).
- Witty, bold, opinionated; strong opening; no em dashes; no hashtags except **#CampaignOfTheWeek**; no clichés or passive voice; check against the humanize-writing skill.
- LinkedIn carries depth; X is punchy. Video posts frame without spoiling and close on "watch till the end".
- Daniel posts and schedules himself. Nothing is posted by Claude.
