# Guest Scout (Questing Desk)

Weekly.
1. Read the Log and pipeline in the tracker. Report the mix: gender, function (Growth/Sales/Product/Ops/Finance/Talent), decision type (Bets/Expansion/Survival/People…), geography, format.
   Oct 2026 baseline: 10 men / 5 women; Growth 5, Sales 4, Product 2, Ops 2, Finance 1, Talent 1; 9 of 16 "Bets"; 15 interviews, 1 breakdown. Ops is the most under-served and highest-value segment.
2. Suggest 2–3 **non-obvious** guests that rebalance the mix, each with the anchor decision to chase and why now.
3. Research each (LinkedIn, public talks, socials) and draft a pitch with 2–3 angles. Daniel sends it.
4. Flag tracker gaps (published editions without URL, scheduled slots without a subject).

## Questing references
- Tracker: Google Sheet "questing-tracker" (Drive id `1fnvKKJ9h6-x1Zz54KxPS6bR5quOV3CgWZ2hddrJR9UQ`, personal account, shared to work). Tabs: Audience, Log, Dashboard, Non-tech Ideas, How to, tech ideas, third (own pieces).
- Recordings: Fathom connector. Interviews can span **more than one recording** (e.g. Charles Isidi: main call + same-day follow-up). Merge transcripts in order before drafting.
- Editorial rules: the decision is the unit; quotes are moments, not lessons; questions are the only headers; no em dashes; no corporate filler; keep the guest's own idioms and self-corrections; intros open on what the subject found; no Rapid Fire; no questions that reference the conversation.
- Production order: anchor decision → headline → subheadline → opener → Q&A → CTA → SEO (title, description, slug, tags) → social (LinkedIn personal, LinkedIn Questing page, X single tweet, Substack Notes). Links go in the post body.
- Publishing: Substack, biweekly.
