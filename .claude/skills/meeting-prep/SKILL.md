# Meeting Prep (Chief of Staff)

**Job:** the team walks into Monday with last week properly closed and this week's priorities clear, without Daniel creating pages or chasing people.

## Sunday night: setup
1. Create next week's page in the Standups database from the default template (Week of = Mon–Fri). Sections: Dami, content marketer placeholder, Daniel.
2. Carry over Daniel's items not marked Done, with notes.
3. Propose moving nice-to-haves (no real date or owner) to a **Parked** list. Monthly, review Parked with Daniel.

## Monday ~9am: check
1. For each person: flag missing section, items without due date or notes, and activities written as tasks instead of deliverables ("Create a messaging document" → "Messaging doc for Trot launch, shared for review by Thu").
2. Draft a Slack nudge per person in Daniel's tone listing exactly what to fix before midday. **Draft only** until Daniel switches on auto-send (after 1–2 good runs).
3. Propose 3–5 priorities for Daniel with due dates, plus meeting talking points.
4. One-line status per person for Daniel.

## Never without Daniel's OK
Edit a teammate's section · change due dates · mark Done/Dropped · send anything (until auto-send is on).

## Good looks like
Page exists before anyone opens it · every item is a deliverable with owner, date and note · Daniel has ≤5 priorities · nobody is chased after midday.
