# Prep + Producer (Questing Desk)

## Before the call: prep
One-page dossier: who they are, the anchor decision to hunt, 8–10 questions that get to "why X over Y", sensitive areas to handle carefully.

## After the call: editor's notes first
Ask Daniel for 5 minutes of notes **before drafting**: how it should open, moments to highlight, what to cut, anything off-record. His journalist's ear decides the shape; the transcript supplies the words.

## Draft
1. Pull every Fathom recording for the guest; merge in order.
2. Run the sensitive-material check (off-record, departures, colleague mentions) and confirm with Daniel.
3. Draft in the production order, following the notes and editorial rules. Preserve the guest's verbatim fragments.
4. Benchmark: the Elizabeth Ajao/Quidax edition (lowest AI-detection score). Avoid too-clean resolutions, parallel structures and smooth transitions.
5. Deliver the SEO set and social set; add the edition to the tracker Log once published.

## Questing references
- Tracker: Google Sheet "questing-tracker" (Drive id `1fnvKKJ9h6-x1Zz54KxPS6bR5quOV3CgWZ2hddrJR9UQ`, personal account, shared to work). Tabs: Audience, Log, Dashboard, Non-tech Ideas, How to, tech ideas, third (own pieces).
- Recordings: Fathom connector. Interviews can span **more than one recording** (e.g. Charles Isidi: main call + same-day follow-up). Merge transcripts in order before drafting.
- Editorial rules: the decision is the unit; quotes are moments, not lessons; questions are the only headers; no em dashes; no corporate filler; keep the guest's own idioms and self-corrections; intros open on what the subject found; no Rapid Fire; no questions that reference the conversation.
- Production order: anchor decision → headline → subheadline → opener → Q&A → CTA → SEO (title, description, slug, tags) → social (LinkedIn personal, LinkedIn Questing page, X single tweet, Substack Notes). Links go in the post body.
- Publishing: Substack, biweekly.
