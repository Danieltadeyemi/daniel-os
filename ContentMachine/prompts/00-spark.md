# The Spark
*Idea-sparring prompts. Use when you have something brewing but can't name it yet. Pick any prompt and just answer — don't overthink it.*

---

## How to Use

Pick a prompt that feels closest to where your head is. Answer it raw — voice note, messy paragraph, bullet points, whatever. The goal is to get the thought out. The machine handles the rest.

If the answer surprises you, it's probably a post.

---

## Growth & Marketing

- What's something most marketers believe that you think is flat-out wrong?
- What's a growth tactic you've seen work that nobody is writing about?
- What's a campaign or decision that looked smart on paper but failed — and what did everyone miss?
- If you had to explain your entire marketing philosophy in 3 sentences to a non-marketer, what would you say?
- What's a question a founder or CEO asked you recently that made you think differently?
- What does "thinking end-to-end" actually mean in practice? Give me an example.
- What's the most common mistake you see marketing teams make when entering a new market?
- What's one thing you know about growth that took you years to understand, and that most people learn the hard way?
- What separates a marketing team that creates real pipeline from one that just creates activity?

---

## Business Insights

- What's a pattern you've noticed across multiple companies or industries that most people overlook?
- What conversation changed how you think about building a business?
- What's something that sounds obvious but is actually hard to execute?
- What's a decision you've seen leaders get wrong repeatedly — and why do they keep getting it wrong?
- What's an assumption most businesses make about African markets that you know isn't true?
- What does a company look like when distribution is undervalued? What are the signs?
- What's something you've learned from watching a company fail that you couldn't have learned from their success?

---

## People & Relationships

- Who did you talk to recently that said something that stuck? What did they say, and why did it stick?
- What's the best piece of advice you received that you initially dismissed?
- What's something about how you work with people that you had to consciously learn — it didn't come naturally?
- Who shaped how you think about leadership? What specifically did they do or say?
- What do you now look for in people that you didn't know to look for earlier in your career?
- What's a conversation from the last month that you keep thinking about?

---

## Family & Life

- What's something being a father has taught you about work that work itself didn't?
- Describe a moment this week with your family that meant something. Don't editorialize — just describe it.
- What's a value you want your daughter to carry? How are you trying to model it?
- What does "bringing your whole self to work" actually look like on a hard day?
- What's something you let go of that you used to think was non-negotiable?
- How do you decide what's actually urgent vs. what just feels urgent?

---

## Worldview

- What's something you believe that the people around you mostly don't?
- What do you think people get wrong about success?
- What's a hill you'll die on — professionally or personally?
- What does "taking your time" look like in practice for you? Give me a real example.
- What's a thing you stopped doing that made you better?
- What's something you've changed your mind about in the last two years?
- Finish this sentence honestly: "Most urgency is manufactured because..."
- What does rest actually look like for you — not the concept, the practice?

---

## Wildcard

- What's on your mind right now that you haven't said out loud yet?
- What would you tell your team tomorrow if you had five uninterrupted minutes?
- What's the thing you keep almost posting but don't?
- If you wrote a post today that you knew only 10 people would read, what would it say?
