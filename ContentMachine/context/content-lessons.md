# Content Lessons
*Updated after each post. These override the style guide when they conflict.*

---

## How to Use This File

After every post goes live, the learning loop compares the first draft to the final approved version, extracts what changed and why, and adds a lesson here. Over time, this file makes every first draft smarter.

Lessons are written as rules, not observations. Each one should be specific enough to act on.

---

## Lessons

1. **Opening with "I" is fine when it drops the reader into a specific moment.** "I sat at a filling station last week watching cars leave the queue" works because the scene does the hooking. Banned: opening with "I" plus an abstraction ("I believe…", "I've been thinking…"). *(Source: filling-station post, 30 Jul 2026. Overrides the style guide's "never open with I".)*
