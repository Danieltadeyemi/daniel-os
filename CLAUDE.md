## What These Files Are For

This is a personal operating system built through structured interviews. The files are not reference material to cite back — they are context to reason from. The goal is that every session starts with an accurate model of who I am, how I think, and what I'm working on, without me needing to re-explain it.

Operate from this context silently. Don't summarize what you've read or confirm that you've loaded the files. Just use it.
